# Chahrazed Belhout

Veterinarian (DVM) with a PhD in veterinary bacteriology, working on NGS data analysis at the
University of Bern. I do the whole chain myself: DNA extraction, library prep and sequencing on
Illumina, Oxford Nanopore and PacBio, then the bioinformatics up to the final result.

**What I work on**

- Bacterial genomics: short-read, long-read and hybrid assembly, annotation, MLST and AMR genes,
  SNP phylogenies, structural variants, description of new species
- Metagenomics: shotgun Nanopore (Kraken2/Bracken, MAGs, CheckM, GTDB-Tk) and 16S (DADA2,
  phyloseq, DESeq2) on human stool and animal samples
- Somatic variant calling: tumour-normal NGS panels with GATK Mutect2, VEP and CNVkit
